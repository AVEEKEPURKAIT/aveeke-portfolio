MY STORY

From digital publishing to performance marketing.

My career began in digital publishing at Patel G.Tech LLP, where I worked as an HTML & XML Executive from August 2023 to March 2025. Structuring content, running quality checks and coordinating with the team taught me to be precise, detail-oriented and process-driven. Those habits matter just as much when building ad campaigns, tracking setups and SEO-friendly websites.

Since October 2025 I have worked in digital and performance marketing at Keyline DigiTech Pvt. Ltd., managing Google Ads, Meta Ads, search engine optimization (SEO), social media, lead generation and client reporting. Alongside my work, I am completing an MBA in Digital Marketing Management at Amity University (2026).

My academic foundation is a Bachelor's degree in Botany from Calcutta University. That science background shapes how I approach marketing: form a hypothesis, run the test, measure the result and improve on it.
