Search engine optimization can feel overwhelming because there is always something else to fix. A clear **SEO framework** solves that: work through the stages in order, and each one makes the next more effective.

Here is the framework I use, from the basics every website needs to the advanced work that builds long-term organic growth.

## Stage 1: Technical foundations

If search engines can't crawl and index your pages, nothing else matters. Start here.

- **Indexing:** Connect Google Search Console, submit an XML sitemap and check the Pages report for errors.
- **Crawlability:** Make sure important pages aren't blocked by robots.txt or `noindex` tags.
- **Site speed:** Compress images, remove unused scripts and test pages with PageSpeed Insights.
- **Mobile experience:** Google indexes the mobile version of your site first, so test every key page on a phone.
- **HTTPS and clean URLs:** Use a secure site and short, readable URLs that describe the page.

## Stage 2: Keyword research

Keyword research tells you what your audience is actually searching for.

1. List the products, services and questions your customers care about.
2. Expand that list with Google Keyword Planner, Semrush or Ubersuggest.
3. Group keywords by **search intent**: informational, commercial or transactional.
4. Map one primary keyword group to each page, so pages don't compete with each other.

## Stage 3: On-page optimization

Once every page has a target keyword, optimize the page around it.

- Write a unique **title tag** (around 50–60 characters) that includes the keyword.
- Write a **meta description** (around 150–160 characters) that encourages the click.
- Use one H1 and organize content with clear H2 and H3 headings.
- Add descriptive **alt text** to images.
- Link to related pages on your own site with meaningful anchor text.

## Stage 4: Content that answers questions

Content is how you rank for the informational searches that bring new visitors. Good SEO content:

- Answers the searcher's question quickly, then goes deeper.
- Is better organized or more useful than the pages already ranking.
- Is updated when information changes.

A simple approach is to build **topic clusters**: one detailed pillar page on a broad topic, supported by shorter articles that cover specific questions and link back to it.

## Stage 5: Authority and off-page SEO

Search engines trust sites that other trusted sites reference. Build authority by:

- Earning links through genuinely useful content, guides and resources.
- Getting listed in relevant industry directories.
- Optimizing your Google Business Profile for local searches.
- Building brand mentions through PR, partnerships and social media.

## Stage 6: Measure and improve

SEO is never finished. Review performance every month:

- **Google Search Console:** impressions, clicks, average position and queries.
- **Google Analytics 4:** organic traffic, engagement and conversions.
- **Rank tracking:** movement for your priority keywords.

Look for pages with high impressions but low clicks (improve the title and description), and pages ranking on page two (improve the content and internal links).

## Final thoughts

The best SEO results come from consistency, not shortcuts. Fix the technical foundations, target the right keywords, create content that genuinely helps, and keep measuring. Follow this framework step by step and your organic traffic can grow steadily over time.
