HOW I USE THEM

The right tool for every stage of marketing.

Tools matter only when they're used with a clear purpose. Here is how each part of my stack supports campaigns.

Advertising platforms

Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads and other paid platforms let me reach people by search intent, interests, profession or behaviour. I choose platforms based on where the target audience spends time and what the campaign needs to achieve.

SEO tools

Google Search Console, Semrush, Ubersuggest and Google Keyword Planner support keyword research, competitor analysis, technical audits and tracking organic visibility over time.

Analytics & tracking

Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Looker Studio measure traffic, conversions and user behaviour, and turn the data into clear reports for decision-making.

Email & outreach

Brevo powers email campaigns and automation, Apollo.io helps with B2B prospect research, and WhatsApp marketing supports direct follow-ups and broadcasts.

AI tools

ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Google AI Studio, Google Flow, Google Vids, Grok and Kling help with research, copywriting ideas, AI image generation and AI video production.

Design & video editing

Canva, Clipchamp and Filmora turn ideas into finished ad creatives, social media posts, reels and promotional videos.
