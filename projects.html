MY PROCESS

How every project is delivered.

Each case study follows the same process, so results can be repeated and improved.

01

Discovery & research

Understanding the business, target audience, competitors and current marketing performance before spending any budget.

02

Strategy & planning

Choosing the right channels, keywords, audiences, budget split and creative direction, with clear KPIs for success.

03

Tracking setup

Setting up conversion tracking, GA4 events, Google Tag Manager and UTM parameters so every lead and sale can be measured.

04

Launch & execution

Building campaigns, publishing content, producing creatives and implementing SEO changes.

05

Optimization

Testing ads, audiences, keywords and landing pages, then moving budget towards what performs best.

06

Reporting

Clear reports on traffic, leads, cost per result and conversions, with recommendations for the next phase.
